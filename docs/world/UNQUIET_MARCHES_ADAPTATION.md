## Purpose

How *The Unquiet Marches* becomes playable inside Vallum Engine. The adaptation
respects the literary intent of the source while translating it into systems:
scenes, routes, party state, moral pressure, dice checks, consequences and
replayable choices.

## Adaptation boundary

The manuscript is the master canon. Vallum is not rewriting the novels. Vallum
creates playable side-structures and vertical slices that let the player
experience the world through bounded decisions. The game may compress, reframe or
stage scenes differently for playability, but must not flatten the core themes
into generic combat fantasy.

## Core adaptation pillars

1. **Violence is capability, not identity.** The player is often capable of force.
   The question is whether force clarifies or corrupts the situation.
2. **Restraint is active.** Not acting is not the same as doing nothing. Restraint
   creates information, cost, delay or future trust.
3. **The world reads reputation before intent.** Kael may reject the name Iron
   Captain, but others still use it. The player experiences the gap between
   self-understanding and public meaning.
4. **Witness matters.** Seeing accurately is a mechanic — asking, observing,
   documenting, waiting, letting another person speak.
5. **Cost arrives through pressure, not lore dump.** The Surge and the Hollow are
   experienced, never explained as cosmology in this line.

## Moral mechanics

- **Force** — immediate capacity to impose an outcome (combat, command,
  intimidation, crisis action).
- **Restraint** — capacity to hold force in reserve when the obvious action is not
  the right one.
- **Witness** — capacity to see accurately and accept what is seen.
- **Hollow** — psychic and moral cost. Rises when Kael acts without asking,
  ignores evidence, uses force as certainty, or performs identity rather than
  choosing action. May also rise when he chooses correctly but cannot avoid cost.
- **Reputation** — how others read Kael. Opens or closes doors, frightens people,
  attracts commissions, distorts testimony, pressures him to become what others
  expect.

## First module: The Noise of Purpose

### Module intent

Adapt the emotional and tactical structure of Book One into a playable session:
pressure, violence, choice, consequence, and the beginning of the Hollow.

### Opening scene structure (the original Ch.1 slice)

- **Ridge above the caravan** — burning caravan below, raiders in the smoke,
  civilians exposed, the raider captain as the tactical centre. Core tension: save
  civilians now or cut the head of the attack.
- **Cut the head** — Force check. Success scatters the raiders but may cost
  civilians; failure creates injury and chaos.
- **Protect the civilians** — Restraint/tactical check. Success saves lives but
  lets the captain reposition.
- **Command the men** — Reputation/Force check. Success coordinates both
  priorities imperfectly; failure exposes the limits of authority under fire.
- **Observe before acting** — Witness check. Success reveals the captain's real
  objective; failure costs time.
- **The Surge** — presented not as a power-up but as narrowing: the world becomes
  clearer and less human at once.
- **Aftermath** — records what was saved, what was lost, what Kael felt, and what
  he refused to name.

The live module continues past this slice through the full Book One arc.

## Required engine capabilities

- non-party objective state (civilians saved/lost, Reshen cost, account lines)
- moral state values, especially Hollow and Reputation
- outcome labels beyond success/failure
- scene-specific stakes panel
- single-character modules
- module metadata for source note and canon status

## Visual direction

The map is an ethical layout, not just a tactical diagram: ridge position, burning
caravan, civilian cluster, raider captain, fire markers, extraction route.

## Writing direction

Narration stays close to Kael's perception: terrain first, feeling indirectly,
action precise, emotion delayed. Stage the facts and let the choice expose the
cost.

## Success criteria

The adaptation succeeds if Phil can play *The Noise of Purpose* and feel that
Kael's world has entered the engine without becoming generic fantasy — finishing
with a record of what was done, saved, lost, and what began inside Kael.
